# Ghent MEDISIP -- monolithic lineage and walk-through PET
location: 51.05, 3.72, Ghent, Belgium
link: https://www.wt-pet.org/

Medical Image and Signal Processing, Ghent University, led by Stefaan Vandenberghe.

The group scales small-animal monolithic detectors to clinical size.

## España et al. 2014 -- DigiPET
link: https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-9155/59/13/3405

Sub-millimetre small-animal PET from thin monolithic LYSO on digital SiPMs.

## Vandenberghe et al. 2023 -- Walk-through flat panel total-body PET
link: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00259-023-06341-x
refs: Pisa

Two flat panels of monolithic blocks, the detector taken from the Pisa block and doubled in every dimension.
